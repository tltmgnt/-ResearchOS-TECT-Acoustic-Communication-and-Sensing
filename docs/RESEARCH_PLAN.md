# Research plan

This independent workspace supplies reproducibility and evidence organization. The included BPSK and correlation algorithms are established baseline techniques; the repository does not claim they are revolutionary or novel.

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow foundation | Valid records, synthetic models, app, reports and evidence bundles | Automated tests and numerical parity | Implemented |
| Differentiated concept | Specific hypothesis and comparison with existing techniques/programs | Literature/program overlap review and independent assessment | Not established |
| Experiment design | Medium, transducers, source/noise calibration, controls and uncertainty | Versioned reviewed protocol | Planned |
| Channel/model validation | Measured propagation/noise and model residuals | Raw data, calibration, repeated trials and uncertainty | No real data |
| Prototype evaluation | Matched baseline and application-relevant performance criteria | Controlled measurements and independent review | Not implemented |
| Engagement | Applicable solicitation, scope and nonduplication review | Current official notice/amendment review | No submission |

Next: define a real scientific question and medium, identify qualified experimental collaborators, quantify uncertainty and detection criteria, and obtain authorized laboratory data. Add environmental, transducer and channel models only with reference benchmarks and validation. Software tests do not establish research novelty or field capability.
