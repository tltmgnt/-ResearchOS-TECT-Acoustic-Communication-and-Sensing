METHOD & RESEARCH CONTEXT

Useful models show their limits.

Idealized communication

Geometric spreading and declared absorption reduce a reference Eb/N0 under fixed-noise assumptions. Unit-energy BPSK symbols are perturbed by Gaussian noise and decided at zero. The model excludes carrier waveforms, multipath, fading, Doppler and synchronization errors.

Synthetic single-echo sensing

A seeded bipolar pulse is injected at a known sample delay. The largest raw correlation score estimates that delay. Range uses known constant sound speed and a round-trip factor of two. No threshold, false-alarm control or environmental validation is implemented.

Independent STO-aligned research

This workspace supports reproducibility and evidence organization for acoustic research concepts. It claims no DARPA affiliation, award, eligibility, scientific novelty or field validation. Review current programs and the actual solicitation before any submission.
